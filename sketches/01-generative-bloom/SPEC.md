# 01 — Generative Bloom

- **Slug**: `01-generative-bloom`
- **Category**: `generative`
- **Status**: Starter Example

## Concept
Layered luminous petals fanning outward with a counter-rotating geometric core and subtle grain texture. Demonstrates immutable composition (`+`, `in_fan`, `spin`, `trail`, `glowing`) and shared studio palettes.

## Visual Direction
- **Palette**: `Studio::Palettes::MIDNIGHT_NEON`
- **Background**: `Studio::Palettes.bg(:night)` (`#070812`)
- **Canvas / Paper**: `[240, 240]`
